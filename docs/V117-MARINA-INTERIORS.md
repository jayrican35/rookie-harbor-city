# V117 seamless Southbay Marina buildings

Dedicated branch: feature/v117-marina-seamless-interiors, based on verified Jay crop fix 8de8d850.

Audit: the existing connected-world canvas already owns movement, circle/rectangle collision, camera follow, RAF timing, NPC arrays and LOOK/E interactions. Marina buildings were flattened approved exterior sprites over full solid rectangles. The office and workshop also inherited original district-wide building solids. Classic interiors are separate maps, so calling their exits would violate continuous-world entry. The classic furniture/room painters inherit precinct-specific native scale and floor overrides; browser inspection confirmed they do not fit these small storefront footprints.

Implementation extends v116-marina.js with four data-driven interior definitions derived from existing exterior objects. No building or artwork is added. The café fits two tables, chairs, a counter, espresso machine, cup shelf, menu, plant and barista. The same definitions furnish the marina office, bait shop and boat workshop with desks/charts, tackle stock and repair tools. Existing world-furniture assets, reception plant artwork, marina plank tiles and NPC animation renderer are reused. Small fitted counters, chairs and equipment are rendered in the existing marina module; the classic precinct overrides are not applied.

The old full-building solids are replaced only for the matching owned building envelopes (including the live renderer’s centered full-height alpha-trimmed bodies). Walls leave real door gaps; furniture contributes its own solid geometry. Doors are passable through continuous collision-resolved walking. Roof alpha follows a time-based ease as Jay approaches/enters and returns when he leaves. The approved exterior shell is painted once in the existing foreground, while the existing entity pass sorts characters and furniture by ground depth. Legacy duplicate decoration painting is skipped for these exact buildings. Offscreen rooms and closed-room actors/furnishings are culled. Existing tree canopies are clipped out of revealed floors so neighboring grove art does not appear inside a shop. The existing precinct draw also returns before preparing its room textures when outside the camera bounds, removing unnecessary first-frame work in the marina. Collision lists are immutable and prepared once.

Interactions reuse existing LOOK/E output and shared actor/spot arrays. Interior props are only selectable while inside their building. They provide ambient inspection and staff dialogue, without changing quest evidence, inventory or saves. Walking dismisses the existing ambient output. The existing camera, mobile stick, RUN control, Jay sprite/animation and campaign-loop suspension are unchanged.

Verification: qa/check-v117-interiors.cjs reuses the campus keyboard/RAF harness with authentic waterfront solids. Jay walks from promenade coordinates through the café door and back, with bounded per-frame travel and unchanged world/map/save state. The same test then runs on the other three buildings. It includes the alpha-trimmed office collision observed in the fully running browser, and negative checks that unrelated districts and small props retain their collisions. Walls, solid furniture, clear door gaps, actor positions, roof reveal/restore, culling and training exclusion are checked. Existing world/road/HUD/NPC/Jay suites pass. Browser checks use the real game at phone portrait and landscape sizes and include exterior/interior screenshots, movement and LOOK interaction. Bounded full-canvas paint timing is exposed through the existing campus API for QA; remote browser timings are not claims about a physical Android device's FPS.

Protected scope: game.js and every art/audio asset, including all Zombie Outbreak content, are byte-identical to the base. No new canvas, animation loop, scene engine, dependencies, networking or save writes are introduced. The main original Site and older test previews remain unchanged; this branch is published at an isolated V117 preview path.
