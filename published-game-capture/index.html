Your background track loops during the main adventure and pauses for Academy courses and cutscene music. Music and sound effects have separate volumes; Sound mutes everything. Captions show scene narration. Settings are remembered on this device.

FIELD GUIDE

Your family adventure: choose Jay, Rosa, Kaleb, Gigi or LingLing. Each character has separate progress on this browser. The children play an Academy simulation about honesty, fairness and responsibility.

Walking: on a phone, hold and drag the joystick with your thumb. Release to stop. On a keyboard, use arrows or WASD. Tap INTERACT or press E near a person or object.

Your next task: open Assignments from Menu or Inventory, or visit the precinct assignment board. Track a task to guide the gold marker. The objective banner shows your current lead.

Story: read chapter briefings, review past decisions and choose an outcome when the evidence is complete. The campaign has thirteen chapters. Later encounters include interviews, document review, safe dispatch handoffs and community planning.

Inventory: use equipment and review collected evidence and statements. Take your issued gear from Locker 7. Equip the uniform and badge; carry your car keys and police radio.

Police Radio: open Inventory → Police Radio. The dashboard’s Police Radio button opens this same screen. At Dana’s traffic stop, choose Run plate HBR-2084 after carrying your radio; no ground marker is needed.

Driving: unlock Unit 9 with your keys, open the door, enter and start the engine. Phone controls change to LEFT/RIGHT steering, GAS and BRAKE; hold BRAKE to reverse. Stop before EXIT. Keyboard: W/S accelerate and brake, A/D steer, G engine, L lights, K siren, M map.

Conversations: tap Next, Done or Continue to return to play. The × closes an ordinary conversation without granting unfinished evidence. Movement controls pause while you use a screen or conversation.

Investigate fairly: hear witnesses out and separate verified facts from guesses. Reopening completed tasks does not award extra points. Later choices affect public trust and the ending.

Keep your progress: the game autosaves on this browser. Export a backup from Menu before changing devices. Use Change player to switch family members.

ROOKIE: Harbor City — a thirteen-chapter family adventure. All people, businesses and records are fictional; no real evidence was misplaced in the making of this game.

    Everything you need. Tap Next in dialogue or press Enter — tap text to reveal it instantly.

    2
    Interact

    Walk to a gold-ringed label and press E or tap INTERACT. People = talk, objects = search, doors = enter, computers = database, car door = drive. The prompt bar always names what you are near.

    3
    Notebook & Evidence

    Findings, witness statements and collected evidence live on the INVENTORY page — tap the INVENTORY button in the top bar (the game pauses while it's open). Check them before concluding a case.

    4
    Map

    Press M or tap MAP. Gold dot = Jay, blue = Unit 9, green star = current objective.

    5
    Drive

    Take the Patrol-Car Keys from your locker, then walk to Unit 9's driver's door (precinct lot) and press E: the vehicle panel opens — unlock, open the door, get in, start the engine. W = gas, S = brake/reverse, A/D = steer, L = lights, K = siren, G = engine, E when stopped = vehicle panel (switch off and step out there). Phone gets its own driving panel. Harbor City now stretches eleven districts — Midtown, Eastside, Northgate, Westfield and Southbay included — so enjoy the longer drives.

    6
    Traffic

    Civilian traffic drives the town's roads in the correct lanes, turns at junctions, and stops at the working traffic lights (Harbor & Main, Southbay, Westfield, Northgate). Watch for brake lights and indicators, and keep your distance — they will. Traffic is solid: a bump only nudges Jay aside or stops Unit 9 with a toast, never damage. With Unit 9's siren on, nearby cars slow and pull over. Stuck cars back up and steer around on their own. Set the amount with the Traffic control in the menu (MENU button → Options; Low / Medium / High).

    7
    Investigate

    Talk (choose questions) → inspect scenes → collect evidence → search the Records database → return evidence to the Evidence Room → interview again with evidence → conclude. A wrong conclusion is explained, never a dead end.

    8
    People

    Harbor City is lived in: civilians stroll the sidewalks, wait at crossings for a safe gap (and for the green at signalised junctions), pause at benches, shopfronts and the bus stop, and stop to watch when Unit 9's siren sounds nearby. Officers walk rounds in the station, workers tend the loading bays, residents potter in their yards. Walk up and press E to chat with some of them — it's optional and never blocks a case. People with a part in a case keep their names over their heads and never wander; townsfolk can't block doors or evidence. And when you drive into a new district, traffic near your arrival point is moved along first — you will never materialise inside another car.

    9
    Cutscenes

    Some story moments play as cutscenes: the camera moves, characters walk to their marks, and dialogue runs in the dialogue panel. Advance with Next / Enter / tapping the text. The Skip Scene button (top-right) jumps ahead without losing any required outcome. When a cutscene ends, control returns to you — try the traffic stop on Harbor Ave East in Midtown: arrival scene, then your investigation, then an outcome scene for the decision you make.
